# Flayora B2B Customer Acquisition Playbook

## Positioning
Flayora is a Puri-based custom printing and merchandise partner for schools, colleges, event organisers, clubs and local businesses. Lead with reliable communication, practical pricing, artwork support and clear delivery commitments—not claims of being the cheapest.

## Priority segments
1. Schools and colleges: annual functions, sports days, farewells, department events, clubs, student groups.
2. Event organisers: festivals, marathons, cultural programmes, religious events, conferences.
3. Local businesses: staff T-shirts, uniforms, branded mugs, caps, keychains, signage and promotional giveaways.
4. Agencies and resellers: overflow production, white-label printing where operationally practical.

## Offer ladder
- Small batch: flexible quantities; setup and handling are reflected in the unit rate.
- Mid-volume: quantity-based pricing after confirming sizes, print dimensions, artwork and deadline.
- Large batch: written production quote with approved sample, size breakdown, delivery schedule and payment milestones.
Never publish a universal ₹/piece rate without specifying garment/material, print size, print method, quantity and delivery terms.

## Qualification checklist
Collect: organisation/contact name, product, quantity, garment/material or product spec, print size/placement, artwork readiness, size breakdown, colour, deadline, delivery location and budget range (optional).
Confirm stock/material availability and sample approval before promising delivery.

## Outreach message — schools / colleges
Hello! This is Flayora, a custom printing and merchandise business in Puri. We help with event T-shirts, caps, badges, mugs and other customised items for college and school programmes. If you have an upcoming event, we can share options and a quantity-based quotation. May I send you our catalogue?

## Outreach message — event organisers
Hello! Flayora in Puri supplies customised event merchandise such as T-shirts, caps, keychains, badges and promotional gifts. We can quote based on quantity, artwork and delivery date. Do you have any upcoming events where we could share a proposal?

## Outreach message — local businesses
Hello! We are Flayora, a Puri-based custom printing partner. We make branded T-shirts, mugs, caps, keychains, signage and promotional merchandise for local businesses. If you're planning staff branding or a promotion, we'd be happy to prepare a practical quotation.

## Weekly prospecting rhythm
- Monday: identify 15 relevant prospects; record contact source and segment.
- Tuesday–Wednesday: send 5–8 personalised first messages per day.
- Thursday: follow up once with prospects who have not replied.
- Friday: prepare quotes, sample photos and close next steps.
- Saturday: ask completed customers for a review/referral and schedule repeat-order follow-up.

Stages: New → Contacted → Replied → Qualified → Quoted → Sample/Approval → Won / Lost → Repeat opportunity.

## Pricing guardrails
- Keep the price agreed with a customer once confirmed.
- Price by order size and operational effort, not by production capacity alone.
- Calculate direct material + variable labour + wastage + packing + delivery + a reasonable overhead contribution.
- For bulk work, confirm whether the buyer supplies garments and whether artwork/setup is included.
- Do not promise the same unit price across materially different quantities or specifications.

## First 30-day targets
- Build a list of 40 qualified local prospects.
- Start 20 personalised conversations.
- Send at least 8 proper quotations.
- Win 2–4 first-time B2B orders (target, not guarantee).
- Ask every completed B2B customer for a review, referral or repeat-order date.

## Important operating note
The website quotation form currently opens a pre-filled WhatsApp message; it does not store enquiries in a database. Do not represent it as a CRM or claim a lead has been saved. Add authenticated lead storage only after server-side authentication and spam protection are implemented.
