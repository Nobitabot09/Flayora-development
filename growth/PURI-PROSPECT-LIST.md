## Outreach sequence

1. Start with the seven event companies: they are the clearest partnership prospects.
2. Ask who handles vendor coordination or merchandise procurement.
3. Send 4–6 strong product photos and a short catalogue, not a giant unfiltered gallery.
4. Ask what event formats they handle most often and what merchandise is repeatedly needed.
5. Offer a small sample/mockup for a qualified opportunity; do not promise free physical samples for every cold lead.
6. Contact colleges through the student activity coordinator, department heads, fest committee or authorised purchase contact. Do not assume a student alone can approve institutional procurement.
7. Follow up once after 3–5 working days. If no response, pause rather than spam.

## First message

Hello! This is Flayora, a custom printing and merchandise business in Puri. We support events with customised T-shirts, caps, badges, mugs, keychains, welcome gifts and signage. We would love to explore becoming a local merchandise/printing partner for your upcoming events. Who would be the right person to speak with about vendor tie-ups?

## Qualification before quoting

Confirm event date, product, quantity, artwork, print size/placement, garment/material, size breakdown, packaging, delivery location and approval timeline. Quote only after these details are reasonably clear.
